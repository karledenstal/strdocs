**Q: Are there cybernetics/cyberware?**
Not in a way that is freely applicable. There are prosthetics that are used to replace limbs and whatnot after traumatic events, but there is no way, as of yet, to easily replace a limb with cybernetics. The prosthetics are advanced enough to respond to your brain activity and thus works like cybernetics, but they're still not advanced enough to completely replace a functioning part of your body.
